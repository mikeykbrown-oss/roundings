Roundings — Privacy Policy

Effective 3 September 2026

Roundings does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • Your race data stays on your device. Boats, ratings, mark roundings and results are saved only in the app's local storage on your phone. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party.
  • No accounts, no tracking. The app has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.
  • Handicap data. ORC certificate figures are bundled with the app and refreshed with app updates. The app does not need a network connection to work.
  • Sharing. Results leave the device only when you choose to copy a CSV or export a PDF.
