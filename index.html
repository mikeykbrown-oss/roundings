Race

Each fleet starts and scores separately, but every boat stays on the one race screen — made for a race bridge running several classes off one line.

Fleets

Rename a fleet by typing in its row. Deleting a fleet moves its boats to the first fleet.

Course marks

Marks are rounded in order; the last mark in the list is the finish. Distance (cumulative NM from the start) is only needed for time-on-distance scoring.

Competitors

Mark one boat as your boat — it becomes the big PING button on the race screen.

Handicap figure

Only knew the wind after the race? Pick the matching certificate figure (e.g. Triple Number … Low / Medium / High) and re-rate — corrected times, the board and the PDF update instantly, before or after the finish. Boats added manually keep their typed rating.
